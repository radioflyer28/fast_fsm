<interfaces>
`AircraftCommands` is a Protocol implemented by slotted `SimulatedAircraft`. `DroneState.on_enter(from_state, trigger, **kwargs)` invokes an optional bound `entry_action`.

`TelemetryPolicy.observe(sample)` stores the latest normalized sample and healthy-heartbeat time; `.sample` and `heartbeat_older_than(seconds)` expose facts only.

`DroneController` owns `_aircraft`, `_telemetry_policy`, and `_fsm`. `update_from_telemetry(sample)` becomes a `TransitionResult`-returning single call to `self._fsm.trigger("telemetry_tick", telemetry_policy=self._telemetry_policy)` after one observation.
</interfaces>
</context>
