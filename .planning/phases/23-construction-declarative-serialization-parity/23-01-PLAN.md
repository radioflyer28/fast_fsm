<objective>
Promote the cold topology model from a singular transition slot to an immutable candidate record sequence, and trace that representation through callable-safe serialization, atomic reconstruction, snapshots, queries, and clones (D-05 through D-08).

Purpose: Establish one candidate-complete identity seam that Phase 23 construction adapters and Phase 24 diagnostics can consume without altering Phase 22's singleton dispatch path.
Output: A complete scalar topology round-trip, explicit condition-reference contract, immutable snapshot rows, coherent target queries, and clone parity.
</objective>

## Representation promotion decision and parity invariant

- **Promote:** `_TransitionSlot` remains the canonical hot storage union, but every in-scope cold consumer must normalize it immediately to one immutable sequence of complete candidate records. The prior singleton-only projection is no longer the topology noun for serialization, construction, declarative identity, clone, snapshot, or query work.
- **Invariant:** for every source/trigger slot, direct registration, serialization round-trip, graph snapshot, target query, and clone expose the same ordered sequence of `(source, trigger, target, priority, condition identity, condition_ref)` candidates; singleton storage is an optimization, not a semantic cardinality assumption.
- Phase 22 selectors retain their direct `TransitionEntry` branch and iterate `slot.entries` directly for groups. The cold helper must never be called from sync or async dispatch.

## Artifacts this phase produces

- A trailing optional scalar `condition_ref` on candidate/prepared/snapshot records.
- One cold immutable candidate-sequence helper used by every in-scope projection rather than singleton rejection or collapse.
- Deterministic one-record-per-candidate `to_dict()` output and validate-resolve-publish `from_dict()` reconstruction.
- Candidate-complete graph snapshots, target queries, and sync/async clone identity coverage.
