<interfaces>
- Historical fields use a closed availability shape: `status=recorded` requires an exact value plus an authoritative source citation; `status=unavailable` requires a reason and searched sources and carries no invented value.
- Required categorical fields are phase, source path, command, build mode, threshold/pass outcome, measurement or counter outcome, environment, and original evidence commit. The parent validator computes source SHA-256 from the final file bytes rather than trusting a self-referential embedded digest. A categorical field may be unavailable when the original record does not establish it; omission of the field or its availability status is invalid.
- Optional `retrospective_rerun` data is separate and requires the new exact command, full execution commit, interpreter/OS/architecture/build mode, UTC execution time, and measurements. It cannot populate original fields or any installed-artifact/performance gate.
- The final Phase 20 installed verifier records exact command/environment/commit/time in its ordinary installed record; D-13's new final proof is enforced in Plan 20-05 rather than retroactively projected onto Phase 16-19 files.
</interfaces>
