Existing contracts to deepen:
- `_PreparedTransition(trigger, sources, target, condition, priority, condition_ref=None, after=None, within=None)` is the frozen/slotted post-normalization carrier.

- `_commit_transition_plan(tuple[_PreparedTransition, ...])` merges replacements off-table, publishes only changed slots, and increments `_graph_version` once.

New private contracts fixed by this plan:
- `_TransitionRequest` is frozen, slotted, private, and retains raw endpoint/value identity in an immutable source tuple until the machine-owned normalizer runs.
- `_apply_transition_requests_owned(requests)` accepts a complete finite collection, validates every element before publication, and is the only request-to-prepared-plan entry point for retained adapters.
