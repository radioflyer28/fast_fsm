From Plan 26-01:
- `_TransitionRequest` is the private raw carrier for every transition-producing adapter.
- `StateMachine._apply_transition_requests_owned(requests)` is the only raw-request normalization/validation/off-table-merge/publication transaction.

Existing adapter contracts to preserve:
- `_add_bidirectional_transition_owned(...)` publishes both directions or neither.
- `_add_emergency_transition_owned(...)` expands all currently registered sources and publishes them as one operation.
- `quick_build(...)`/`quick_fsm(...)` preserve caller-supplied `State` object identity while auto-creating unresolved string states locally.
- `from_dict(config, conditions=...)` validates dictionary shape and transition-indexed field context, resolves opaque condition references, builds a private candidate, and returns it only after complete topology success.
- `FSMBuilder.add_transition(...)` currently rejects malformed priority/timing/guard graphs before staging and performs async classification; `build()` assigns `_machine` only after candidate topology and callbacks succeed.
- `_clone_owned()` preserves shallow identity for canonical State/Condition/callback/listener collaborators but must rebuild independent transition table, slot, and entry containers through the canonical transaction; Phase 30 alone owns parity for its future final/internal/rejection metadata.
